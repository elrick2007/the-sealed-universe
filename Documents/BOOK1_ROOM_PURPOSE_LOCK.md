# Book 1 Room Purpose Lock

Last updated: 2026-06-15

This document locks the remaining mapped rooms before Blender architecture replacement. It does not add new routes by itself. Its job is to make sure every room on the Book 1 maps has a gameplay reason, an evidence reason, or a deliberate reason to stay inaccessible.

Game 01 remains Book 1 only. The canon ending is the publish/send register route that resolves Mara as `Incomplete` and leads into Book 2. Pen and oil can remain as witnessed offers, but they are not alternate true endings.

## Room Categories

- **Critical Route**: required for the Book 1 ending spine.
- **Required Evidence**: required before the ending can feel earned, but not always a lock-and-key puzzle.
- **Optional Evidence**: strengthens the evidence board, Living Ledger, and anthology texture without changing the ending.
- **Atmosphere / Read-Only**: supports dread, navigation, or visual storytelling without adding a puzzle chain.
- **Structural / Locked**: exists for navigation, contradiction, or later acts, but should not be treated as an unsolved room.
