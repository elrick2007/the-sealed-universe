# THE WEEPING WALLS — POST-CREDITS EPILOGUE
## "There Is Always One" · 45–60 seconds · image + dialogue only · plays after all three endings

**Design rules:** No gameplay. No explanation. Claymation style locked to the series' master style string. The stinger is ending-agnostic except for one inserted variant line (§3). Book 2's era stays deliberately ambiguous — no dates, no technology, nothing that fixes when Sera's story happens relative to Mara's. The connective tissue is the ink and the refusal, not the calendar.

---

# 1 · THE SCENE

**BLACK.** Over black: water, dripping, deep — the Well Room's acoustic, which the player knows in their bones by now.

**IMAGE 1 — The Well.** Lamplight. The windlass rope creaks taut and begins to wind, alone. Something rising from the dark water. *(Claymation: macro on the rope's fibres straining, droplets falling back into black.)*

**IMAGE 2 — The List.** The preserving jar, raised; in hands we know — Mara's, ink-stained — the paper list unfolds: place-names across five continents, each with its small drawn symbol — a house, a tent, a tower, a ship, a tree. **Five symbols circled in fresh ink that is not hers.** The camera drifts past all of them to a sixth mark, top of the page, older than every other entry, the paper worn soft around it: **a moth, drawn around a mountain.** Uncircled. First on the list. Waiting longest.

**DIALOGUE — VOICE 1** *(through the recorder's speaker — that thin, captured quality; a girl's voice, young, accent unplaceable, calm as ritual)*:
> "The sun returns. We return to see it."

*(Beat. The recorder's red LED reflects in the jar glass.)*

> "You have lived sixteen years in question. Today you will see."

**IMAGE 3 — The Cup.** Dissolve: a clay cup on smooth ceremonial stone, dawn light on mountains we have never seen in this game. The cup is full of ink — black-blue, iridescent as a beetle's wing, containing depth that cannot fit inside a cup. In its surface: the reflection of a girl's face. The face is not looking at the sky behind it. It is looking at us.

**DIALOGUE — VOICE 2** *(the dictating male voice from the attic void — measured, patient, filing)*:
> "Item: one cup. Disposition: **refused.**"

The ink ripples once — a single ring, from no touch.

**SMASH TO BLACK. TITLE in hand-pressed clay letters:**
> **THE INK DWELLERS**

*(Hold three seconds. The room-tone hum, one breath of it, then silence.)*

---

# 2 · WHY THESE PIECES

- **The Well + the jar list** are already canon (Vol 4) — the stinger spends assets the player has earned, not new exposition.
- **Voice 1's lines are Book 2's opening liturgy verbatim** — when readers/players reach Book 2 (or Season 2 of the series), the recognition lands retroactively.
- **Voice 2 (the void's dictator)** gets exactly one more line in the entire franchise here. Book 2's Memory Keeper saw a figure with eclipse-light for a face deliver the first vial; the dictating voice can *eventually* be that phenomenon — but in this game it stays unexplained. One line, no answers. The void's design law holds.
- **The moth-and-mountain symbol** retro-seeds the list: it should be quietly present on the jar list from the game's launch (players who screenshot the Well Room reward in Act 4 will find Book 2 was always first on the page). Cheap now, legendary later.
- **The Quiet Archive thread:** Book 1 already contains the bridge line — Eleanor's journal, July 2nd 1887: *"a library where the old records were kept, before the fire. Whitmore said: what fire? Thomas said: exactly."* **Patch note:** make sure this exact journal entry is one of the page-turn beats in the Vol 5 witnessing scene, and that the Foundation Chamber book's early pages include one entry referencing "the ash that was gathered." The player who connects manor-archive to burned-library before Book 2 announces it has earned the franchise's best theory-thread.

# 3 · ENDING VARIANTS (one inserted line each, between Image 2 and Image 3)

**After PUBLISH** *(canonical)* — Eleanor, dry, warm, from nowhere:
> "She isn't the only one who refused, you know. She was just the first one you met."

**After SERVE** — no voice; instead the ledger, onscreen, writing itself in the merged hand:
> *"New correspondence received. File under: beginnings."*

**After DESTROY** *(the NG+ frame)* — no well, no jar; the list arrives anyway: slid under the closed cellar door on the same sheet of paper the player posted in Act 1 — returned at last, the moth-and-mountain drawn on it, nothing else. *(In the reset house, only the list crosses over. The house keeps the version that wasn't witnessed — but the network remembers.)*

# 5 · THE FRANCHISE RULE THIS ESTABLISHES
Each book = one complete game (or season) with its own contract honoured in full; each ends with one borrowed minute from the next — an image, a voice, a refusal. Book 2's game/season will end the same way, reaching toward Book 3. The series' name for these can even be diegetic: **correspondence.** The keepers write to each other. So do the books.
